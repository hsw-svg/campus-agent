# 导师机器人

- 生成方式：内置 `image_gen`
- 用途：学生端“星图学习舱”导师透明角色资产
- 参考：`ai-companion.webp` 仅用于统一高品质 3D 渲染语言，不复用其头部、身体、姿态或配件

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a completely new teacher mentor robot for a university student dashboard. Taller broad-shouldered synthetic robot with a calm horizontal eye display, layered hard-surface shell panels shaped like an academic mantle, one hand guiding and the other holding an open holographic book with abstract diagrams and no text. Polished stylized 3D animation-film character render, trustworthy, patient and authoritative, clearly non-human. Full character from head to upper thighs, three-quarter view, centered with generous padding and a dignified silhouette. Ivory white, deep navy, restrained warm gold and cyan. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, cast shadow, contact shadow or reflection. Render on a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background with no gradient, texture, floor or lighting variation; do not use magenta on the subject.
