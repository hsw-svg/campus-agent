# 默认学伴机器人

- 生成方式：内置 `image_gen`
- 用途：学生端“星图学习舱”默认 AI 学伴透明角色资产
- 参考：`ai-companion.webp` 仅用于统一高品质 3D 渲染语言，不复用其头部、身体、姿态或配件

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a completely new default AI learning companion robot for a university student dashboard. Balanced all-purpose synthetic robot with a compact oval head, cyan eyes, modular white and deep-navy shell, cyan navigation light on the chest, one hand presenting a small holographic compass made only from abstract geometry. Polished stylized 3D animation-film character render, clearly non-human, approachable and capable. Full character from head to upper thighs, three-quarter view, centered with generous padding and a readable silhouette. Cool studio rim light. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, cast shadow, contact shadow or reflection. Render on a perfectly flat solid `#ff00ff` chroma-key background with no gradient, texture, floor or lighting variation; do not use magenta on the subject.
